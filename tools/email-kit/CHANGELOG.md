# Usha changelog

Each release updates the same installed app. Newest first.

## 3.0.0 (2026-11-03)

- Approved local agency profiles and channel permissions
- Safer conversation closure, calendar reminders and CRM handoff
- Separate account records from reusable recruitment content

## 2.0.0 (2026-11-02)

- Client records with history that stays after you close Usha
- Follow-ups, postponements, meetings and opt-outs now follow clear rules
- First emails rebuilt from your own email, with 60-day replacement and a negotiable fee

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-02)

- Mark as sent now clears the form for your next prospect
- Closed conversations and Do not contact checks are stricter
- The tracker and Do not contact list now share one panel

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-02)

- Added a tracker that shows which follow-ups are due
- Added WhatsApp drafts, calendar reminders and opening drafts in your email app
- Added a stop line for cold messages and spam checks

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-02)

- Usha is now an installable app that works offline
- New versions install themselves into the same app
- Added backup and restore for the Do not contact list

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-02)

- Renamed the tool to Usha and redesigned the interface
- Added ten response categories, subject reuse and reminder limits
- Added Do not contact handling and auto-fill

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-02)

- First offline outreach composer for Example Search Agency
