# Recruitment mandate (one page): template

> Have this reviewed by a lawyer before first use. The details in braces come from the brief.

**Between** Jobboard300 ({registered entity and address, to be confirmed}) and **{client legal name}** ({address, GSTIN}).

1. **The role.** {title}, {location / work mode}, CTC {range}, {number} opening(s). Brief agreed on {date} with {client contact, role}. The client authorises Jobboard300 to advertise and recruit for this role.
2. **What Jobboard300 does.** It sources and screens candidates, and submits each one with a note and with the candidate's consent. It arranges interviews and supports the offer and joining.
3. **Fee.** {7.5–8.33}% of the hire's first-year fixed annual CTC, plus GST. It is invoiced on the joining date and payable within {15/30} days. No fee is due unless a candidate Jobboard300 submitted joins within 12 months of the submission.
4. **Replacement.** If the hire leaves or is let go for performance within 60 days of joining, Jobboard300 finds a replacement at no extra fee. This does not apply after a role change or a redundancy.
5. **Candidates.** The client uses candidate data only for this hiring purpose, does not share it, and deletes the CVs of candidates it does not hire. It does not approach candidates it has received outside this process.
6. **Non-solicitation.** For {12} months Jobboard300 will not approach a person placed with the client about other jobs.
7. **Term.** This mandate applies to the role(s) above until filled or withdrawn in writing. Further roles can be added by email reference to this mandate.

Signed for the client: ______ (name, role, date)  Signed for Jobboard300: ______ (name, date)

*After signing:* in the owner console, Job portal → Clients → **Record signed mandate** (date and where the signed copy is kept). On the role → **Record authorisation** (who, and when).
