# Employer outreach (Bengaluru pilot)

The proposition is narrow and checkable: **a screened shortlist of three for one role, within ten working days, and a fee only if your hire joins.** Do not promise anything the [readiness page](INVESTOR_READINESS.md) does not show you can do.

## Email (to the founder or hiring manager; quote their own posting)

Subject: Your {role} opening: three screened candidates, fee only on joining

Hello {first name},

I saw {company} is hiring a {role} ({posting link}). I run Jobboard300, a small Bengaluru recruitment service for {family} roles at growing companies.

For one role, I would send you three candidates I have personally screened against your brief, with a short note on each covering notice period, current and expected CTC, and why they fit. You interview only the ones you want. The fee is {7.5–8.33}% of the first-year salary, and only if your hire joins. If they leave within 60 days, I replace them at no charge.

Could we take 15 minutes this week to agree the brief? {call link or two time options}

{name}, Jobboard300 · {phone}

## Follow-up (4 working days later, once)

Hello {first name}, a short follow-up on your {role} opening. If it is still open, I can send three screened profiles within ten working days of a 15-minute brief. If you have already filled it, congratulations, and I will not write again about this role.

## Call script: agreeing the brief (15 minutes)

1. What must the person achieve in the first 90 days?
2. What is mandatory, what is preferred, and what can be trained?
3. CTC range, location and work mode, notice period you can wait for, and joining date.
4. Who interviews, how many rounds, and how fast can you give feedback? (Agree 48 hours.)
5. Agree the fee, the 60-day replacement and the invoice timing. Send the [mandate](MANDATE_TEMPLATE.md) the same day.
6. Ask who will authorise the vacancy in writing (name and role). Record it on the vacancy before you publish anything.
