# Recruitment services agreement (template)

**Status: draft for your lawyer.** This template states the commercial terms the
product enforces. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must be before it is
signed. Fill in the bracketed fields for each client.

**Between** [Jobboard300 legal entity name], [registered address], Kolkata, India ("Jobboard300")
**and** [Client legal name], [registered address], GSTIN [number] ("the Client").
**Date:** [date]

## 1. Services
Jobboard300 will search for, screen and submit candidates for the roles the
Client gives it in writing or through the hiring workspace, and will arrange
interviews. The Client makes all hiring decisions.

## 2. Fee
1. The placement fee is **[7.5 to 8.33]%** of the candidate's annual salary as
   stated in the offer letter (fixed pay plus guaranteed allowances; variable
   pay is excluded unless guaranteed).
2. The fee becomes payable on the day the candidate joins. Jobboard300 invoices
   on joining, and the Client pays within **[30]** days of the invoice date.
3. GST is charged in addition at the prevailing rate.
4. There is no charge for shortlists or interviews.

## 3. Replacement
1. If a placed candidate leaves, or is dismissed for performance or conduct,
   within **60 days** of joining, Jobboard300 will provide **one** replacement
   for the same role free of charge.
2. The replacement applies only if the fee was paid on time and the Client told
   Jobboard300 within **[7]** days of the departure.
3. It does not apply where the departure results from redundancy, a material
   change to the role or its terms, or a breach by the Client.
4. No refund is due in place of a replacement.

## 4. Introduced candidates
If the Client or a group company engages a candidate introduced by Jobboard300,
in any role, within **12 months** of the introduction, the fee in clause 2 is
payable.

## 5. Candidate information and confidentiality
1. Candidate profiles and CVs are confidential. The Client uses them only to
   decide on the role in question.
2. The Client sees a candidate's profile while its hiring process with that
   candidate is open, and contact details from the offer stage.
3. The Client will not copy, share or keep candidate information beyond that
   purpose, and will delete it when the process ends or when Jobboard300 passes
   on a candidate's request.
4. The Client does not receive access to Jobboard300's candidate database,
   scores, test results or selection method.
5. Each downloaded CV carries a watermark identifying the Client. The Client is
   responsible for copies downloaded under its accounts.

## 6. Tests (optional)
Where the Client opts for Jobboard300's aptitude test, or supplies its own
questions, Jobboard300 runs the test for candidates in the Client's process.
Charges, if any: **[included / amount]**. Test levels and answers remain with
Jobboard300.

## 7. Non-solicitation
For **[12]** months after a placement, Jobboard300 will not approach the placed
person about other roles.

## 8. Data protection
Each party complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its
rules as they apply to it. The Client will tell Jobboard300 without delay if
candidate information is lost or disclosed without authority.

## 9. Term and termination
This agreement runs from its date until either party ends it with **[30]** days'
written notice. Clauses 2, 4, 5 and 8 continue to apply to candidates already
introduced.

## 10. Liability
Jobboard300 screens candidates with reasonable care but does not warrant a
candidate's performance. The Client is responsible for its own reference,
background and document checks. Jobboard300's total liability under this
agreement is limited to the fee paid for the placement concerned.

## 11. Law and disputes
This agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Kolkata have
jurisdiction.
