**Regulatory facts that bite on contract staffing**
- **Contractor licence:** the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions (OSH) Code took effect on 21 Nov 2025 and absorbed the Contract Labour Act. A contractor needs a licence at **50 or more contract workers** (previously 20). The licence is valid for 5 years, and a single all-India licence is possible. **M–H** ([LKS](https://employmentlaw.lkslaw.com/labour-codes/osh), [Counselence PDF](https://counselence.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CEC-Labour-Code-Overview-of-the-OSH-Code-November-28-2025.pdf))
- **GST:** 18% applies to permanent placement (SAC 998512) and contract staffing or manpower supply (998513). Executive search is SAC 998511. **M** ([cleartax](https://cleartax.in/s/support-services-gst-rates-sac-code-9985))

### Recommendation for Job Point
- **Now:**
  - Permanent placement of IT mid-level and junior roles. Charge 8.33% at entry level and 10–12.5% for niche or mid-level, with a 60–90 day replacement guarantee and invoicing on joining.
  - Fresher and campus hiring for local Durgapur and Kolkata colleges.
  - Pre-screening plus a resold or partnered BGV, priced as a pass-through plus a margin.
- **Next 3 months:**
  - Interview-as-a-service using vetted freelance senior engineers.
  - Small-scale C2H. Put the conversion terms in the contract from the start, for example a free conversion after 6 months on billing, or 1 month's CTC if earlier.
- **Later:**
  - Contract staffing. You become the employer: you need PF/ESI registration and working capital to pay salaries before clients pay, and the OSH Code licence once you reach 50 contract workers.
  - Light RPO for one anchor client.
- **Avoid:**
  - US bench sales. It needs a different market, different visa and tax knowledge, and different time zones.
  - Executive search, until you have a track record.

## 2. What clients expect from an agency's client portal

### Findings (vendor documentation; mostly H for "the feature exists", L for quality)
- **Zoho Recruit:** clients review shortlisted candidates, give go/no-go feedback (a "Client Review" record), and interviewers submit feedback forms in the portal. Admins control who sees feedback. There are separate client, vendor and candidate portals, and the client portal reportedly needs higher-tier plans. ([Zoho collaborative hiring](https://zstatic.zohostatic.com/recruit/collaborative-hiring.html), [Zoho Reviews](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/community/topic/introducing-the-reviews-sub-module-in-zoho-recruit?page=2), [recruiterslineup](https://www.recruiterslineup.com/top-client-portal-software-for-recruitment-and-staffing-agencies/))
- **Recruit CRM (Hiring Manager Portal):** the client signs up once. The recruiter controls which candidates and which fields the client can see, per candidate or per stage. Clients leave feedback and change stages, and changes sync in real time. ([help.recruitcrm.io](https://help.recruitcrm.io/en/articles/8943042-share-jobs-with-your-hiring-manager), [receiving feedback](https://help.recruitcrm.io/en/articles/1466788-receiving-feedback-from-clients))
- **JobAdder:** a branded portal for job requisitions, submissions and interview feedback. Recruiters see when a CV has been viewed. Managers rate candidates and leave notes, and pick their available interview slots ("Event Availability"). ([JobAdder client portal](https://jobadder.com/platform-feature/client-portal/), [hiring manager portal](https://jobadder.com/platform-feature/hiring-manager-portal/))
- **Manatal (Guest Portal):** clients review shortlists, comment and approve. It can be white-labelled with your logo and custom domain, and clients can create job openings. ([manatal.com](https://www.manatal.com/blog/recruiting-crm), [G2](https://g2.com/products/manatal/features))
- **Bullhorn:** clients accept or reject submissions, through a portal or a unique email link. Third-party add-ons (Candidately) offer no-signup links and client-response-time metrics. ([Bullhorn KB](https://kb.bullhorn.com/ats/Content/BHATS/Topics/understandingBHHiringProcess.htm))
- **Loxo:** clients comment, score and approve, and can export a PDF status report. ([help.loxo.co](https://help.loxo.co/en/articles/11049207-using-loxo-s-hiring-manager-portal))
- **Ceipal (Procurewise):** a vendor management system with timesheets, auto-invoicing and multi-level approvals. I could not confirm a client-facing review portal. ([trustradius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/ceipal-procurewise))

### Table stakes versus nice-to-have
This split is my analysis, based on which features recur across these vendors.

| Table stakes (MVP) | Nice-to-have (later) |
|---|---|
| Secure per-client login or magic link, with multiple users per client company and roles (viewer / reviewer / admin) | White-label and custom domain |
| Shortlist per job, with recruiter-controlled field visibility (mask candidate phone and email until the client approves) | CV-viewed tracking and client response-time analytics |
| Approve / reject / hold with structured feedback (reason codes plus a comment) | Client-created requisitions |
| Live pipeline stage view per job | Exportable PDF or Excel status reports, scheduled by email |
| Interview scheduling (client offers slots) and an interview feedback form | Invoices and payment status in the portal |
| India-specific: offer and joining tracker showing offer date, notice period, last working day (LWD), expected joining date, joined/no-show, and the replacement-guarantee clock | Timesheets (only if you do contract staffing) |
| An audit trail of who saw which candidate (also supports data-protection accountability) | Market or salary benchmarks per role |

## 3. Indian IT hiring realities

### Findings
- **Notice periods:** 90 days is the norm for confirmed staff at large IT services firms (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech). Typical ranges are 15–30 days at entry level and 30–60 days for mid-level individual contributors. No national law sets notice periods; the contract does. **M**. These are consistent secondary sources; I found no primary company policy. ([instahyre](https://resources.instahyre.com/blog/notice-period-in-india/), [hyring](https://hyring.com/free-hr-toolkit/hr-glossary/notice-period-90-days-india))
  - Claims that "67% of IT firms mandate 90 days" (attributed to NASSCOM 2024) and "90% shorten 90-day notice to 30–45 days" (from the vendor Hush) are **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Notice buyout:** usually (monthly gross or basic ÷ 30) × unserved days. Whether gross or basic is used depends on the contract. **M**
- **Offer drops and no-shows:** I found no solid primary India-IT figure. Reported numbers:
  - About 33% of candidates who accepted offers backed out before joining in Q3 2024. This is a Communications Today survey cited in an opinion piece ([Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/hans/young-hans/an-integrity-pact-can-effectively-address-indias-offer-dropout-problem-1007580)).
  - 25–35% per month, a Taggd claim made in the same piece.
  - 28% of accepted tech offers, from Teksands' internal data ([teksands](https://teksands.ai/blogs/india-tech-hiring-playbook-2026)).
  - About 37%, from AxureOne, which cites "industry estimates".
  - A counter-offer loss of 20–30% during notice is **UNVERIFIED**.
  - Working assumption: **25–35% of accepted offers fail to convert to joining. L–M**
- **Time to fill:** Quess "IT Workforce Trends in India 2025" gives 45–60 days on average and 75–90 days for AI/ML and cybersecurity. The notice period adds 30–90 days on top before the person joins. **M** ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/tech/indias-it-job-demand-touches-18-million-in-2025-led-by-gccs-product-firms), [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/economy/india-s-it-job-market-grows-by-16-in-2025-quess-corp))
- **Interview process:** this comes from Glassdoor reports, so it is anecdotal. **M**
  - An online assessment (aptitude and/or 2–3 data-structures-and-algorithms coding questions).
  - 1–2 technical rounds (live coding, SQL, projects, system design for senior roles).
  - A managerial or techno-managerial round.
  - An HR round (salary, notice period, relocation).
  - Then the offer and BGV.
- **BGV practice and law:**
  - Standard checks cover identity, address, education, employment, criminal records (eCourts) and references. **M**
  - **UAN/EPFO-based employment checks** use the Universal Account Number of the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation. They show employer names and tenure but **not designations**, and only Indian employment. They are run with the candidate's written consent. **M** (vendor sources only: [ongrid](https://ongrid.in/blogs/what-is-uan-based-employment-verification/), [salarybox](https://salarybox.in/blog/what-your-employees-uan-number-reveals-about-their-real-work-history/)). I found no EPFO primary rule on third-party access: **UNVERIFIED**.
  - **Legal basis under the DPDP Act:** section 7(i) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act allows employment-related processing without consent. Commentators are split on whether it covers *candidates*, and the safest reading is to rely on consent under s.7(a)/s.6 for pre-joining BGV. **M** ([Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/privacy/employee-background-verification-and-pre-employment-screening-under-the-dpdp-act), [Chambers](https://chambers.com/articles/navigating-the-legitimate-use-exemption-for-employee-data-under-the-digital-personal-data-protection))
- **Moonlighting:** Wipro dismissed about 300 staff found working for rivals (Rishad Premji, Aug–Sep 2022). Infosys confirmed firing employees for dual employment, while allowing approved gig work (Oct 2022). **H** ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/wipro-found-300-staff-working-with-rivals-at-same-time-stand-by-my-comment-on-moonlighting-rishad-premji-news-224833), [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/economy/infosys-does-not-support-dual-employment-have-fired-violators-in-last-12-months-salil-parekh/1166823))
  - That overlapping PF contribution periods reveal dual employment is my inference, not a sourced method. I found no 2025–26 policy update from either company.

### Recommendation: what Job Point's system should track
- For each candidate:
  - Contractual notice period, negotiated LWD, buyout possibility or amount, and whether the employer will reimburse it.
  - Competing offers and their stage, plus a counter-offer risk flag.
  - Current and expected CTC, and preferred locations (Kolkata, Durgapur, Bengaluru, remote).
- Interview rounds, each with a structured feedback score.
- Offer date, acceptance date, and **joining-confirmation checkpoints** (for example at T-30, T-14, T-7 and T-1 days before joining), then joined or no-show, and the replacement-guarantee window.
- Time-to-fill by stage: requisition → shortlist → interview → offer → join.
- BGV consent record (versioned notice, timestamp, scope) and per-check status. Store only the result, not raw EPFO data.
- Offer-drop reason codes, so you can quote your own drop rate to clients. This would be a selling point, given how poor the public data is.

  - Xpheno reports a median tenure of **1.4 years** for Indian software engineers (article about 20 months old, so roughly early 2025). Business analysts, frontend and backend developers, QA, AI/ML and cybersecurity roles are shorter; data engineers, SDETs and product managers are longer. **M** (search extract only; [Xpheno blog](https://blogs.xpheno.com/how-often-do-indian-software-engineers-switch-jobs/))
  - Randstad's 2025 global survey (11,250 workers, 15 markets) puts average tenure in the first five years of a career at **1.1 years for Gen Z**, versus 1.8 for Millennials, 2.8 for Gen X and 2.9 for Boomers. **H** ([Randstad](https://www.randstad.com/press/2025/genz-workplace-blueprint/))

  - Blind (May 2025, 2,712 verified professionals in India): 68% tried to change jobs in the past 12 months. **M**
- **The "18 months after joining" claim:** no source measures when Indian IT professionals start looking. **UNVERIFIED.**
  - Indirect evidence fits, if you treat 18 months as when people *leave* rather than when they start looking. A 1.4-year median tenure (about 17 months) and a 60–90 day notice period imply that job searching typically starts around **12–15 months** after joining. That is my inference.
- **Early-career versus mid-career:** early-career tenure is clearly shorter (Xpheno; Randstad 1.1 vs 1.8 years). Reports say GCCs draw talent with under 5 years' experience away from IT services firms (May 2025). I found no attrition breakdown by years of experience: **UNVERIFIED**.

### Recommendation for Job Point
- Schedule consented career check-ins at about **9–12 months** after placement, and again around the April–June appraisal season. That appraisal timing is my assumption, not sourced.
- Run a lighter check-in at 6 months for freshers and people with 0–3 years' experience.
- Never time an approach to a placed candidate inside the client's non-solicit period (see Q6).

## 6. Keeping placed candidates "warm"

### Findings
- **Industry practice:** staffing firms run "talent communities" combining content, job alerts and automated engagement. The aim is redeployment and passive-candidate reach (Bullhorn's "Connected Recruiting"). Zoho promotes the same idea. I found no sourced cadence for check-ins. **M** ([Bullhorn](https://www.bullhorn.com/blog/unlocking-the-power-of-talent-community-to-maximize-redeployment/), [Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/blog/recruit/talent-communities-for-staffing-agencies.html))
- **DPDP limits:**
  - Consent must be specific and withdrawable, so job alerts and newsletters need their own itemised consent, separate from processing a CV for a current job.
  - The Act sets **no fixed retention period** for recruiters. Data must be erased when consent is withdrawn or the purpose ends (s.8(7)).
  - The 3-year inactivity rule in the Third Schedule applies only to large e-commerce, gaming and social-media platforms. **M–H** ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/data-retention-and-deletion-under-the-dpdp-act-how-long-can-businesses-retain-personal-data-in-india/1844062), [JSA](https://www.jsalaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/JSA-Prism-InfoTech-November-2025-DPDP-Rules.Final_.pdf))
  - Practitioner advice: candidate talent pools need their own consent and a defined review period. 180-day deletion of rejected candidates is cited as practice, not law. **M**
- **Approaching a candidate you placed:**
  - Recruitment and staffing templates commonly bar the agency from soliciting the client's employees or placed candidates for **about 12 months** (sometimes 12–24). **M**
  - Under s.27 of the Indian Contract Act, *post-employment non-competes* on employees are void. Non-solicitation clauses are treated more leniently. Wipro v. Beckman Coulter (Delhi HC, 2006) held that non-solicitation is not void in itself, and Indian courts view **6–12 months** as reasonable. **M** ([Desai & Diwanji](https://desaidiwanji.com/insights/articles/validity-of-non-solicitation-and-non-compete-clauses-in-employment-agreements), [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=3b6ded21-2615-4d36-827d-cb8712a3a5f9))
  - Commentary on business-to-business no-poach clauses is split under s.27. Separately, the Competition Commission of India (CCI) opened its first no-poach cartel probe (Givaudan, DSM-Firmenich, IFF; Suo Motu Case 02/2025, Aug 2025). The Delhi HC refused to stop it on 23 Feb 2026, and the appeal was dismissed on 15 Apr 2026. The merits are undecided. **H** ([Delhi HC judgment](https://delhihighcourt.nic.in/app/showFileJudgment/59623022026CW25272026_161309.pdf), [indiacorplaw](https://indiacorplaw.in/2026/08/13/the-reach-of-indian-competition-law-in-addressing-no-poach-agreements/))
  - The practical point: a bilateral agency non-solicit is a contractual risk you should honour. It is not something to test in court.
- **WhatsApp Business Platform:**
  - Marketing messages need prior opt-in. Since Nov 2024 the opt-in no longer has to mention WhatsApp by name. Promotional content must use *marketing* templates; misusing utility templates breaks Meta policy. Opt-outs must be honoured, and blocks and reports lower your quality rating and messaging limits. **M** (secondary sources; official policy at whatsapp.com/legal/business-policy was not opened)
  - India rates (Meta list price per delivered message, 2026): marketing **₹0.8631** (up from ₹0.7846 on 1 Jan 2026), utility and authentication **₹0.115**, plus 18% GST. Business service providers (BSPs) add markups. **M** ([chatmaxima](https://chatmaxima.com/whatsapp-api-pricing/india/), [whautomate](https://whautomate.com/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india)). Meta's own rate card was not opened.
- **Email:** Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (about 5,000+ messages a day to Gmail) require SPF, DKIM and DMARC, one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) honoured within about 2 days, and a spam rate below 0.3%. **M**
- **Calls and SMS:** TRAI's TCCCPR rules on commercial communications were amended on 12 Feb 2025. Promotional calls must use the 140 number series, service and transactional calls the 1600 series, and ordinary 10-digit numbers are barred for telemarketing. **H** ([TRAI press release 11/2025](https://cms.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/PR_No.11of2025.pdf))

### Recommendation for Job Point
- Collect three separate tick-boxes:
  - (a) processing for the current application
  - (b) talent pool or future roles
  - (c) newsletters, learning tips and job alerts by WhatsApp or email
- Re-confirm consent every 12 months. Purge or anonymise anyone inactive for 24 months or who withdraws. These periods are my suggestion, not law.
- Add a "client non-solicit lock" to each placement. It blocks *outbound job pitches* to that person for the contract period (default 12 months) but still allows consented learning or community content. Do not initiate re-placement during the lock; if the candidate approaches you, log that.
- Use WhatsApp utility templates only for transactional messages (interview reminders, joining checklists). Use marketing templates only for people who opted in.

### Recommendation for Job Point
- Seed the skills taxonomy from **ESCO** for skills and occupations, with **O\*NET** "Hot Technologies" for tool names. Both are safe for commercial use with attribution.
- Use the SSC NASSCOM role titles from NQR for *Indian role names only*, typed in by hand, until reuse terms are confirmed.
- Use Stack Overflow tech names as a cross-check only. Do not ship a derived database, because of ODbL share-alike.
- Avoid Lightcast unless you pay for a licence.
