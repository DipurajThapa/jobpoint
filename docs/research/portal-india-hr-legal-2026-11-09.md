## 1. How Indian job portals charge for CV and contact access

### Findings

**Naukri (Resdex)**
- Naukri does not publish a price for full Resdex. Its buy page shows "Custom price / Contact sales" ([internshala](https://internshala.com/blog/employer-naukri-resdex-price/), [culturro](https://culturro.com/hr-software/ai-sourcing-screening/naukri/)).
- Self-serve prices seen by third parties:
  - **Resdex Lite: ₹4,000 for 100 CV views**, about ₹40 per CV (seen 12 Aug 2026).
  - **Small-business plan: ₹3,500 + GST for 30 days**, with 60 CV views and 2 job posts (checked 21 Oct 2026, [hirin.ai](https://www.hirin.ai/blog/naukri-resdex/)). Older sources say ₹2,500 for the same plan ([internshala](https://internshala.com/blog/employer-naukri-resdex-price/)).
- **Viewing the same CV again is free for 90 days.** Quota is deducted only if no user in your company has viewed that CV in the last 90 days. The check is company-wide, and the super-user can set weekly or monthly caps per sub-user under "Manage Quota" ([Naukri FAQ](https://company.naukri.com/landing-page/naukri_faq/new/recruiter/resdex.html), [recruiterfaq.naukri.com](https://recruiterfaq.naukri.com/category/resdex-database/how-to-use-resdex-basic-features/), [Naukri blog](https://www.naukri.com/blog/recruiter-resdex/)).
- There is a free demo of Resdex that lets you search but hides contact details.
- Confidence: High on the model and the 90-day rule; Medium on prices.

**foundit (formerly Monster India)**
- Prices are by quote only. foundit's terms treat "Resume Database Access" as a separate paid service ([foundit plan dashboard](https://recruiter.foundit.in/edge/user-management/planDashboard/?plan=custom), [foundit ToU](https://www.foundit.in/info/terms-of-use)).
- One third party estimates ₹30,000–₹1.2 lakh a year ([internshala](https://internshala.com/blog/employer-monster-job-posting-price/)). This is UNVERIFIED.
- Confidence: Low.

**Shine**
- One blog lists about five packages, from roughly ₹4,999 (30 days) to ₹22,999 (90 days). Each includes unlimited search, with profile views rising by tier ([internshala](https://internshala.com/blog/employer-how-to-post-job-on-shine-com/)).
- No official rate card was found, and I found nothing on whether a re-view is charged. Both are UNVERIFIED.
- Confidence: Low.

**Apna** (official help centre)
- Unlocking a profile costs 1 database credit. Unlocking plus an Excel download costs 2 credits ([Apna help](https://employer-help-centre.apna.co/support/solutions/articles/1060000120761-what-are-the-charges-for-viewing-the-profile-phone-number-and-cv-using-credits-)).
- Credit packs: ₹3,190 for 450 credits over 90 days, and ₹9,990 for 1,500 credits over 360 days ([cashaly](https://www.cashaly.com/store/apna-coupons)). That works out to about ₹7 per credit, but the source is about 2 years old.
- Confidence: High on the credit model; Low on prices.

**WorkIndia**
- Plans are 30, 90 and 365 days, with **100, 300 and 800 database unlocks**. The unlock counts appear on WorkIndia's own pricing page ([workindia.in/pricing](https://www.workindia.in/pricing/)).
- The most recent prices found are ₹2,350, ₹4,999 and ₹11,000, about ₹23.5, ₹16.7 and ₹13.75 per unlock ([f6s](https://www.f6s.com/software/eloquentinfosolutionsprivatelimited-workindia)).
- Older listings disagree: ₹4,200 and ₹7,500, with 200 and 350 unlocks ([flexiple](https://flexiple.com/reviews/workindia)).
- Confidence: Medium.

**Instahyre** (official pricing page)
- **Free plan:** an employer can view 6 profiles a week, capped at 3 per recruiter.
- **Premium job:** ₹2,500 + tax per job for 30 days, unlocking 250 profiles (junior tech or any non-tech role), about ₹10 per profile.
- Database subscriptions are by quote ([instahyre.com/pricing](https://www.instahyre.com/pricing/), [help.instahyre.com](https://help.instahyre.com/en/article/job-slots-access-and-features-6w96vi/)).
- Confidence: High.

**iimjobs / hirist**
- Prices are by quote. One profile uses 1 CV access, and further actions on that profile are covered for 90 days ([iimjobs FAQ](https://www.iimjobs.com/faq), [hirist contact](https://recruit.hirist.tech/contact-us)).
- Confidence: Medium.

**Hirect**
- Freemium model, metered by chats per day: 5 (free), then 15, 30 or 100 on paid plans. Verified recruiters get a 3-day trial at 30 chats a day ([Hirect T&C](https://www.hirect.in/termsconditions/)).
- INR prices: UNVERIFIED.

**Indeed India (Smart Sourcing)**
- Professional plan: ₹33,000 a month or ₹3,15,000 a year for 100 contacts a month, with extra contacts at ₹330. Contacts roll over and expire after 6 months ([in.indeed.com/hire/resume-search](https://in.indeed.com/hire/resume-search), [culturro](https://culturro.com/hr-software/ai-sourcing-screening/for-india/)).
- Another Indian Indeed page shows a Standard plan at ₹8,000 a month for 30 contacts.
- Confidence: Medium.

**Patterns across portals**
- Mass-market and blue-collar portals (Apna, WorkIndia, Instahyre's premium job) charge **₹7–₹25 per unlock**.
- White-collar database products (Naukri Lite, Indeed) charge **₹40–₹330 per contact**.
- Credits almost always sit with the **company account**, with an admin able to cap individual users.
- Re-viewing an already-unlocked CV within a window (usually 90 days) is free.
- Validity is tied to the plan: 30, 90 or 365 days.
- Free access usually means search or preview without contact details, or a small weekly quota.

### Recommendation for our build
- **Free allowance:** unlimited anonymised previews, plus **5 free unlocks** once the first job description is approved, usable within 30 days, one-time per company. This mirrors Instahyre's free weekly profiles and Hirect's trial.
- **Tiers.** Prices are indicative and shown **excluding GST**, which is how Indian B2B portals quote ("+ GST", "+ tax"). I did not verify the GST rate for job-portal services here; 18% is the common rate for online B2B services, but check with your CA.

| Tier | Unlocks | Validity | Price (excl. GST) | Per unlock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 100 | 90 days | ₹7,999 | ~₹80 |
| Growth | 250 | 180 days | ₹17,999 | ~₹72 |
| Pro | 500 | 365 days | ₹29,999 | ~₹60 |

  These sit between Naukri Lite (₹40) and Indeed (₹330), which fits a product that sells pre-screened precision.
- **Re-views:** free for the rest of the plan's validity, or 180 days, whichever is longer, for anyone in the company.
- **Quota scope:** one pool per company, with per-user caps set by the company admin, as Naukri does.

## 2. Indian HR data conventions for CV and JD fields

### Findings

**How CTC is expressed**
- CTC (cost to company) is quoted in **LPA, lakhs per annum**: 5 LPA means ₹5,00,000 a year.
- Recruiters separate **fixed** pay (guaranteed) from **variable** pay (performance-linked, sometimes including stock).
- CTC includes employer PF and gratuity (about 4.81% of basic), which the employee never receives monthly. Monthly in-hand pay is CTC minus employee PF (12% of basic), professional tax and TDS ([keka](https://www.keka.com/lpa-salary), [weekday](https://www.weekday.works/post/ctc-meaning-in-hand-salary), [bajajfinserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/what-is-ctc-meaning-and-full-form-in-salary)).
- Blue-collar and grey-collar portals, and tier-2 salary data, quote **per month** instead (for example, Indeed's Durgapur figures below).
- Confidence: High.

**Notice periods**
- There is no legal standard; the contract decides. 30–90 days is typical.
- By employer type: startups 15–30 days, mid-size product companies 30–60, large IT services firms 60–90 (TCS, Infosys, Wipro and HCL typically 90 for confirmed staff), multinationals and GCCs 30–60 ([instahyre](https://resources.instahyre.com/blog/notice-period-in-india/), [goodspace](https://goodspace.ai/blogs/888538/notice-period-in-india-meaning-rules-calculation), [omnivoo](https://omnivoo.com/blog/notice-period-buyout-india)).
- Buyout is usually (monthly gross ÷ 30) × days not served. Whether it is calculated on basic or gross pay changes the cost two to three times. The largest IT firms often restrict buyouts.
- Resdex filters by notice period, including "currently serving notice" ([Naukri Recruiter Zone](https://recruiterzone.naukri.com/page/9)). I could not verify the exact filter labels.
- I found **no independent national survey** with average notice periods by sector. A figure of "63% of senior IT staff serve 60–90 days" comes from a single consultancy blog ([wsne](https://wsneconsulting.com/blog/notice-period-buyout-guide-india-2026)) and is UNVERIFIED.
- Confidence: Medium.

**Seniority bands**
- Resdex searches use an experience range, a CTC range, keywords and location ([Naukri Recruiter Zone](https://recruiterzone.naukri.com/tag/naukri-resdex)).
- I found no official standard set of bands. Your bands (fresher, 0–2, 2–5, 5–10, 10+) are a reasonable convention, but no source verifies them.

**Pay in Durgapur and tier-2 versus metros**
- Durgapur, from Indeed (salaries in job posts):
  - Sales executive: **₹16,787 a month** (range ₹10,845–₹25,985, n=42) ([Indeed](https://in.indeed.com/career/sales-executive/salaries/Durgapur--West-Bengal)).
  - Accountant: **₹16,178 a month** (n=20) ([Indeed](https://in.indeed.com/career/accountant/salaries/Durgapur--West-Bengal)).
  - HR executive: about ₹20,000–₹24,000 a month from Glassdoor, but based on only 2–3 salaries ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/durgapur-west-bengal-hr-executive-salary-SRCH_IL.0,20_IC2902444_KO21,33.htm)).
- Metro comparison:
  - National average fresher salary is 3.22 LPA, with Mumbai at 4.29 and Delhi at 4.05 (FY25, [getwork](https://getwork.org/blog/fresher-job-market-india)).
  - Metros pay a **15–20% premium** over tier-2 cities ([India Employer Forum](https://indiaemployerforum.org/world-of-work/non-metro-cities-as-emerging-tech-hubs-in-india/)).
- Bands for each seniority level in Durgapur or Asansol: **UNVERIFIED**. No source had them.
- Confidence: Medium for the entry-level Durgapur figures; Low beyond that.

**NCO-2015 (government occupation codes)**
- About 3,600 occupations with **8-digit codes**, built on NCO-2004 and ISCO-08. The last 2 digits link to Qualification Pack / National Occupational Standards (QP-NOS) ([NCS Vol I](https://www.ncs.gov.in/Documents/National%20Classification%20of%20Occupations%20_Vol%20I-%202015.pdf), [DGE](https://dge.gov.in/dge/national_classification_of_occupations)).
- It has not been revised since 2015. A revision is "under consideration" (NCVET, May 2025, [report](https://ncvet.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Report-on-Mapping-of-Qualifications-with-NCO-Codes.pdf)).
- Confidence: High.

**NSQF (Skill India qualification levels)**
- Revised and notified on 6 June 2023, aligned to the National Credit Framework. It has 8 levels with half-steps (2.5, 3.5, 4.5, 5.5, 6.5) ([NCVET](https://ncvet.gov.in/?p=11295), [teamlease](https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/44419/national-skills-qualification-framework-nsqf-2023)).
- Confidence: High.

**Open skills taxonomies**
- I found **no open Indian skills taxonomy or API**. Skill India Digital Hub has job and course discovery and verified credentials, but no published skills ontology ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2039515)).
- **ESCO (EU)** has about 3,000 occupations and 14,000 skills, with a free API and no key needed ([ESCO](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/ar/use-esco/use-esco-services-api)). The licence for the data itself was not verified.
- **Lightcast Open Skills** has 33,000+ skills, but its free tier is for individual and non-profit use, capped at 50 extraction calls a month, and excludes occupations ([Lightcast docs](https://docs.lightcast.io/lightcast-api/docs/free-api-access), [FAQ](https://lightcast.io/open-skills/faqs)).

### Recommendation for our build
- **Pay fields.** Store whole rupees per year for current fixed, current variable and expected CTC. Display in LPA. For roles below about 3 LPA, also accept and display a monthly in-hand figure.
- **Notice period.** Use these options: Immediate, ≤15, 30, 45, 60, 90, >90 days. Add a "serving notice" flag with the last working day, and a "buyout possible" yes/no.
- **Experience.** Store years and months; work out bands for display: Fresher (<1), 1–3, 3–6, 6–10, 10–15, 15+.
- **Taxonomy.** Use NCO-2015 only as an optional mapping for reporting, at the 4-digit family level. It suits ITI and vocational roles (relevant to Durgapur's industrial employers), but it is stale and too coarse for modern white-collar titles.
- **Matching.** Match on our own curated role list plus skills seeded from ESCO, since its API is free. Check ESCO's data licence first.
- **Qualifications.** Record NSQF level as a qualification attribute for vocational certificates.

## 3. DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025

### Findings

**When the Rules were notified**
- G.S.R. 846(E), dated **13 November 2025**. Some sources say 14 November, which is the upload stamp; the Gazette text is dated the 13th ([SS Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/meity-notifies-final-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025/), [bitscore](https://www.bitscore.in/instruments/dpdp-rules-2025), [india-briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/dpdp-rules-2025-india-data-protection-law-compliance-40769.html)).
- Confidence: High.

**When each rule takes effect** (per Rule 1, across several consistent sources)

| Rules | Starts | Date |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 2, 17–21 (setting up the Data Protection Board) | On publication | 13 Nov 2025 |
| 4 (registering consent managers) | After 1 year | **13 Nov 2026** (some say 14 Nov) |
| 3, 5–16, 22, 23 (notice, security, breach, retention, children, rights, Significant Data Fiduciaries, cross-border) | After 18 months | **13 May 2027** |

- The May 2027 date is calculated, not printed in the text ([protectcomply](https://protectcomply.com/blog/dpdp-rules-2025-timeline), [maheshwariandco](https://www.maheshwariandco.com/blog/dpdp-consent-manager-framework-november-2026/)).
- The Act's own duties on withdrawal of consent also apply from 13 May 2027 ([Cyril Amarchand FAQ](https://www.cyrilshroff.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FAQs-DPDPA.pdf)).
- Confidence: High.

**Pending changes to the timeline**
- In January 2026 MeitY proposed cutting the 18 months to 12, which would mean 13 Nov 2026. Reports differ on whether this covered everyone or only Significant Data Fiduciaries. **It had not been notified** as of September 2026 sources ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/meity-may-cut-compliance-timeline-for-key-dpdp-rules-to-12-months-126012201293_1.html), [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/privacy/compression-of-dpdp-enforcement-timeline-proposed-by-meity)).
- **Check the e-Gazette this week**, because 13 Nov 2026 is four days away.
- The **DPDP (Removal of Difficulties) Order 2026** (S.O. 5458(E), 5 Oct 2026) only fixes wording in s.9(1) and s.10(2)(c)(ii). It changes nothing of substance ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/10/15/dpdp-removal-of-difficulties-order-2026/), [SS Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/dpdp-removal-of-difficulties-order-2026-what-has-been-changed-for-childrens-data-and-sdf-audits/)).
- Confidence: Medium. Status after September could not be confirmed.

**Rule 3 — what the notice must contain**
- It must be a standalone notice that can be understood on its own.
- It needs an **itemised description of the personal data** and the **specific purpose** for each item, plus the goods or services involved.
- It must give a link or means to **withdraw consent**, as easily as consent was given, to **exercise rights**, and to **complain to the Board** ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/), [tsaaro](https://tsaaro.com/blogs/building-transparent-user-experiences-consent-notice-standards-under-the-dpdpa-and-dpdp-rules-2025), [konfirmity](https://www.konfirmity.com/blog/dpdp-consent-notice-requirements)).
- Confidence: Medium. I could not read the exact wording.

**Withdrawing consent (the Act)**
- s.6(4): withdrawal at any time, as easy as giving consent.
- s.6(5): the candidate bears the consequences, and processing done before withdrawal stays lawful.
- s.6(6): the fiduciary must cease, and **make its Data Processors cease**, within a "reasonable time". No fixed deadline is set.
- s.8(7): erase the data when consent is withdrawn or the purpose ends, and make processors erase it too ([apnilaw s.6](https://www.apnilaw.com/bare-act/dpdp/section-6-digital-personal-data-protection-act-dpdp-consent/), [dpdpactindia](https://dpdpactindia.in/dpdp-act/section-6/), [taxclue s.8](https://taxclue.in/blog/section-8-dpdp-act-2023-erasure-and-retention-of-personal-data/)).
- Confidence: High.

**Rule 8 and the Third Schedule — does the 3-year erasure and 48-hour warning apply to us?**
- The 3-year inactivity erasure, with a warning at least **48 hours** before, applies only to three classes:
  - e-commerce entities with ≥2 crore registered users in India
  - online gaming intermediaries with ≥50 lakh users
  - social media intermediaries with ≥2 crore users
  ([seclore](https://www.seclore.com/fundamentals/dpdp-rules-2025-compliance-guide/), [sansalegal](https://www.sansalegal.com/post/how-to-build-a-data-retention-and-erasure-schedule-under-the-dpdp-rules-2025))
- **It does not apply to our portal.** A job portal is not a listed class, and even read as "e-commerce" we are far below 2 crore users. The general purpose-based erasure in s.8(7) applies instead.
- Confidence: High.

**Rule 8(3) — one-year minimum retention (applies to everyone)**
- **All** Data Fiduciaries must keep the personal data, associated traffic data and logs of processing for **at least one year from the processing**, for the Seventh Schedule purposes (requests from the State under s.36). After that, erase unless another law requires keeping it.
- Rule 6(1)(e) separately requires keeping logs for one year for security.
- Commentators note this cuts against storage limitation, and one example says it applies even after an account is deleted ([NALSAR TLF](https://techlawforum.nalsar.ac.in/privacy-with-a-footnote-data-retention-under-the-dpdp-framework/), [mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1844062/data-retention-and-deletion-under-the-dpdp-act-how-long-can-businesses-retain-personal-data-in-india), [barandbench](https://www.barandbench.com/view-point/meity-notifies-final-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025)).
- Confidence: Medium-High.

**Rule 7 — reporting a breach**
- To **each affected candidate, without delay**: the nature, extent, timing and likely consequences, and what they can do.
- To the **Board, without delay**, then a **detailed report within 72 hours** (extendable on request).
- There is no minimum severity: every breach is reportable ([konfirmity](https://www.konfirmity.com/blog/dpdp-breach-notification), [matters.ai](https://www.matters.ai/article/dpdp-breach-notification)).
- CERT-In's separate 6-hour cyber-incident reporting also applies. I did not verify its text in this pass.
- Confidence: Medium-High.

**Data processors (vendors who handle data for us)**
- We remain liable whatever the contract says (s.8(1)).
- Processors may be engaged only under a contract (s.8(2)).
- Rule 6(1)(f): the contract must contain specific security safeguards. A generic "industry-standard security" clause will not satisfy it ([tsaaro](https://tsaaro.com/blogs/data-security-safeguards-under-the-digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023-(dpdpa)-and-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025), [sansalegal](https://www.sansalegal.com/post/how-to-specify-reasonable-security-safeguards-under-rule-6-of-the-dpdp-rules-2025)).
- Confidence: Medium-High.

**Significant Data Fiduciaries**
- There is **no numeric threshold**. The s.10(1) test weighs several factors (volume, sensitivity, risk, sovereignty and others), and designation is by Gazette notification.
- **No designations had been made** as of mid/late 2026 ([KSK](https://ksandk.com/data-protection-and-data-privacy/significant-data-fiduciaries-dpdp-act-compliance-guide/), [pricoris](https://pricoris.com/blog/significant-data-fiduciary-dpdp-act/)).
- Confidence: Medium-High.

**Children**
- s.9 and Rule 10 require verifiable parental consent for anyone under 18. Being adults-only takes us out of Rule 10, but we still need a reasonable age gate.
- I found no source specifying what that age gate must be (UNVERIFIED).

**Cross-border transfer**
- s.16 works as a "negative list": transfers are allowed except to countries the government restricts. **No countries have been notified.** Section 16 and Rule 15 take effect around May 2027. Stricter sector rules (for example RBI) still apply ([KSK](https://ksandk.com/md/data-protection-and-data-privacy/cross-border-data-transfers-dpdp-act-india/), [mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1764976/from-localisation-debates-to-a-negative-list-making-cross-border-data-transfers-work-under-indias-dpdp-act)).
- Confidence: Medium-High.

### When a candidate withdraws consent after a company has unlocked their CV

**The portal's obligations**
- Stop processing and stop showing the candidate to anyone.
- Erase the CV and profile, and make our processors (hosting, email, CV parser) do the same (s.6(6), s.8(7)).
- Sharing that happened before withdrawal remains lawful (s.6(5)).
- Keep the minimum logs and traffic data for one year under Rule 8(3).
- Keep a record of **which companies received the data**. Under **s.11(1)(b)** the candidate can ask for "the identities of all other Data Fiduciaries and Data Processors with whom the personal data has been shared" ([apnilaw s.11](https://www.apnilaw.com/bare-act/dpdp/section-11-digital-personal-data-protection-act-dpdp-right-to-access-information-about-personal-data), [watchdogsecurity](https://watchdogsecurity.io/dpdp/right-to-access-information)).

**The company's position**
- Once it unlocks a CV, the company decides its own purposes (shortlisting, contacting), so it is most likely a **separate Data Fiduciary** under the s.2(i) "determines purpose and means" test.
- A secondary summary says Naukri's recruiter terms treat recruiters as "independent data controllers" ([hirin.ai](https://www.hirin.ai/blog/naukri-resdex/)).
- The Act has **no clear joint-fiduciary regime** ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/09/24/joint-fiduciaries-under-the-dpdpa-the-invisible-risk-in-indias-financial-data-ecosystems/)) and does not deal with transfers from one fiduciary to another ([Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2023/global/decrypting-indias-new-data-protection-law-key-insights-and-lessons-learned)).
- **I found no provision, and no source, that obliges the portal to pass a withdrawal on to independent fiduciaries.** The only statutory duty to pass it on is the duty towards processors.
- Whether the employer can rely on the employment exemption in s.7(i) for job applicants is disputed ([KSK](https://ksandk.com/data-protection-and-data-privacy/employee-background-verification-dpdp-act/), [IndusLaw/Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1591018/handling-resume-submissions-under-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-act)).
- **Caveat: I found no law-firm opinion on exactly this portal scenario.** Confidence: Medium on our obligations; Low on the company's.

### Recommendation for our build
- Build to the 13 May 2027 standard now, and check whether the 12-month cut gets notified.
- **Show the candidate one itemised notice that names "sharing with approved employers who unlock your CV" as a distinct purpose.**
- **On withdrawal:**
  1. Remove the candidate from all previews immediately.
  2. Hard-delete the CV and profile within 7 days.
  3. Move only the minimum logs to a locked store, purge them after one year, and confirm this with counsel.
  4. **By contract, require each company that unlocked the CV to delete its copy within 15 days of our notice, and send that notice automatically.**
- Keep an unlock ledger (company, user, date) so we can answer s.11 requests.
- Ask for date of birth with an 18+ declaration.
- Host in India (Mumbai, ap-south-1).
- Get a short opinion from Indian counsel on the company's status as a separate fiduciary and on Rule 8(3).

## 4. Email and SMS for an Indian platform

### Findings

**SMS — TRAI DLT registration**
- What has to be registered:
  1. The business as a **Principal Entity** on an operator's DLT portal (Jio, Airtel, Vi or BSNL)
  2. A **header** (sender ID)
  3. Each **content template**, with `{#var#}` placeholders
  4. A **link to the SMS provider's telemarketer ID**; one source says messages are blocked without it
- Every message must then carry the Entity ID and Template ID ([AWS docs](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/registrations-sms-senderid-india-specify-ids.html), [telerivet](https://www.telerivet.com/blog/india-sms-compliance-trai-dlt-registration-and-tcccpr-guide), [medium](https://abhishekjoshi-dev.medium.com/from-dlt-registration-to-sms-delivery-building-a-fully-compliance-indian-sms-gateway-e89e87fe5b86)).
- Detailed rules:
  - Transactional headers are alphabetic and exactly 6 characters in India.
  - Each template variable can be at most 30 characters.
  - The text must match the template exactly (spacing, punctuation, case) or operators block it ([AWS](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/registrations-sms-senderid-india.html)).
- Documents: PAN, GST, incorporation proof (CIN), signatory ID, address proof and a letter of authorisation. AWS also lists TAN.
- Fee: **₹5,000 + tax (₹5,900) for one year** per Airtel's FAQ. Most sources give the same for Jio and Vi; BSNL is uncertain at ₹3,540 or ₹5,900 ([smsgatewayhub](https://crm.smsgatewayhub.com/knowledge-base/article/dlt-registration-charges-cost-breakdown-in-india), [ojiva](https://www.ojiva.ai/blogs/dlt-registration-charges-india/)).
- Timelines (vendor estimates): entity 1–3 working days, header 1–3 days, templates 1–2 days. **Plan for about 2 weeks.**
- Links in SMS must be whitelisted on DLT (cited from 1 Oct 2024; the original TRAI direction was not verified).
- The TCCCPR Second Amendment (12 Feb 2025) changed the template categories. Header suffixes (-P/-T/-S/-G) come from a single source and are UNVERIFIED ([sigmachambers](https://www.sigmachambers.in/post/2025-tcccpr-amendments-a-renewed-push-by-trai-for-order-in-commercial-communications-1), [MEF](https://mobileecosystemforum.com/2025/07/11/in-india-trai-tougher-on-spam-a-call-for-industry-action/)).
- Confidence: Medium-High on the process; Medium on fees.

**SMS providers**

## 5. Pre-employment assessments in India

### Findings

**AMCAT**
- Core modules: English, Quantitative and Logical, plus a Personality Inventory.
- Reports give a **percentile for each section**.
- **Scores are valid for 1 year.** Candidates can **retake after 45 days**, and employers usually see the **latest** score ([myamcat](https://www.myamcat.com/help/solution/how-many-times-can-i-take-the-amcat-exam/35829), [faceprep](https://faceprep.in/article/amcat-results-score-report-and-feedback-report-card/)).
- Confidence: Medium-High.

**SHL**
- Scores are percentiles against a **norm group the employer chooses**, with sten and T-score equivalents.
- Results are commonly kept 12 months; retake waits are set by the employer, often 6–12 months. These come from prep sites only ([careertestprep](https://www.careertestprep.com/knowledge/shl-retake-policy), [iqmetrics](https://iqmetrics.org/news/what-is-a-good-shl-test-score-percentiles-and-norm-groups/)).
- Confidence: Low-Medium.

**Aon (cut-e)**
- Retake rules are set by the employer. No published validity period ([claveprep](https://claveprep.com/blog/aon-cut-e-assessment-test-guide-2026)).
- Confidence: Low.

**Mercer Mettl** (anti-cheating)
- A kiosk/lockdown browser that disables extra tabs, copy/paste, right-click and developer tools, and logs attempts to leave the test window.
- Proctoring options: AI-only, recorded with human review, or live human.
- A "Credibility Index" report is mentioned by one candidate guide only ([mettl](https://mettl.com/en/secure-proctor/)).

**HackerEarth** (anti-cheating)
- Random webcam snapshots.
- Full-screen mode with warnings for switching tabs, and automatic logout after about 5 switches.
- A Smart Browser, and plagiarism checks against the web and past candidates ([HackerEarth help](https://help.hackerearth.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002987633-Proctoring-settings), [HackerEarth](https://www.hackerearth.com/recruit/features/proctoring)).
- These are vendor claims.

**iMocha (AI-EnglishPro)**
- Tests reading, writing, speaking and listening, and maps results to CEFR levels Pre-A1 to C2.
- Uses a safe browser and identity verification ([iMocha help](https://help.imocha.io/how-to-read-ai-englishpro-reports), [iMocha](https://www.imocha.io/blog/understanding-cefr-levels-a1-to-c2)).

**Naukri**
- Resdex has a "Candidates with verified skills" filter that shows which assessment backs each skill ([Naukri Recruiter Zone](https://recruiterzone.naukri.com/introducing-candidates-with-verified-skills-in-resdex/)).
- How long Naukri scores stay valid, and its retake rules: UNVERIFIED.
- For comparison, a LinkedIn badge requires a top-30% score, and you can retake once within 6 months ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a507663)).

**Legal and fairness constraints**
- **RPwD Act 2016:** s.20 bans employment discrimination. Its express duty to provide accommodation is on government establishments, but the courts have pushed reasonable accommodation further ([factohr](https://factohr.com/rpwd-act-2016/), [leglobal](https://leglobal.law/2026/09/01/non-discrimination-and-reasonable-accommodation-at-the-private-workplace-understanding-employer-obligations-towards-persons-with-disabilities/)).
- ***Gulshan Kumar v IBPS*** (Supreme Court, 3 Feb 2025, 2025 INSC 142): a scribe or extra time cannot be limited to candidates with a "benchmark" disability of 40% or more ([SC Observer](https://www.scobserver.in/supreme-court-observer-law-reports-scolr/facility-of-scribe-for-persons-with-disabilities-gulshan-kumar-v-institute-of-banking-personnel-selection/), [Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/news/litigation/supreme-court-allows-all-disabled-candidates-opt-scribes)).
- s.21 requires an equal opportunity policy. Whether the 20-employee threshold is a mandate is disputed: UNVERIFIED.
- **No Indian statute on language bias in tests was found** (UNVERIFIED).
- Confidence: Medium.

### Recommendation for our build
- Make candidate assessments **optional, and use them only to boost ranking**, with a cap, never to exclude anyone.
- Report each score as a **percentile against our own norm group**, showing the group size and date.
- **Scores valid for 12 months.** Allow a **retake after 30 days, at most 3 times a year**, with the **latest score counting** (the AMCAT approach).
- Offer an accommodation request flow (extra time or a scribe-compatible mode) with no disability-percentage threshold.
- Do not apply English scores to roles that don't need English, and offer tests in Hindi and Bengali.
- Mention proctoring video and images in the DPDP notice.

## 6. Rules against bypassing the platform and poaching candidates

### Findings

**Naukri's terms**
- Naukri may act against anyone who "copies, misuses, transmits or crawls" data for any purpose other than a bona fide one ([naukri.com/termsconditions](https://www.naukri.com/termsconditions)).
- According to a secondary summary of the Resdex rules, recruiters must:
  - contact candidates only about genuine jobs
  - never charge candidates a fee
  - not forward a CV to the candidate's current employer
  - act as an independent data controller
  ([hirin.ai](https://www.hirin.ai/blog/naukri-resdex/))
- **I could not get the verbatim recruiter agreement.**

**foundit's terms**
- India: the employer "shall not use this Service to source candidates or to contact Jobseekers... in regards to business opportunities prohibited by these Terms" ([foundit.in ToU](https://www.foundit.in/info/terms-of-use)).
- Philippines terms: no use of the resume database except for hiring, "including... to sell or promote any products or services" ([foundit.com.ph](https://www.foundit.com.ph/info/terms-of-use)).
- foundit also disclaims responsibility for copies that employers keep.
- Clauses on scraping or bulk download: UNVERIFIED.
- Confidence: Medium.

**Consultancy agreements in India**
- Fees, usually calculated on **fixed** CTC, plus GST:
  - entry level: 8.33% (one month's pay)
  - mid level: 8.33–12.5%
  - senior: 12.5–16.67%
  - retained search: 20–33%
- **Replacement guarantee of 60–90 days**, usually replacement only, so check whether the clock starts at offer or joining.
- **Candidate ownership of 6–12 months** (sometimes 180 days), to catch "back-door" hires.
- Payment due 15–30 days after the candidate joins.
- ([hyring](https://hyring.com/blog/recruitment-agency-fees-india/), [savannahr](https://savannahr.com/blog/recruitment-agency-fees-india-how-pricing-works), [hirekey](https://hirekey.in/blog/it-recruitment-agency-contract-terms-india-what-to-check/))
- Confidence: Medium (market norms, not legal sources).

**Can these clauses be enforced?**
- Under s.27 of the Indian Contract Act, narrow non-solicitation clauses between businesses have been upheld (*Wipro v Beckman Coulter*, Delhi High Court, 2006) ([casemine](https://www.casemine.com/commentary/in/enforceability-of-non-solicitation-clauses-in-distributor-agreements-under-section-27-of-the-indian-contract-act:-wipro-ltd.-v.-beckman-coulter-international-s.a./view)).
- Whether a fee for hiring a candidate directly can be enforced: UNVERIFIED. It likely turns on contract-damages law (ss.73–74), so get counsel's view.

### Recommendation for our build
- **Terms for companies should include:**
  - use only for hiring the company itself
  - no resale or sharing
  - no scraping or automated access
  - no charging candidates
  - no shared logins
  - delete CVs of candidates not hired within 90 days, or on our withdrawal notice
- **"Introduction" clause:** if the company hires a candidate it saw in a preview within **6 months** without unlocking them, it pays the unlock cost plus a fee of **8.33% of fixed CTC**.
- **Technical protections:** strip identifying details from previews (no name, no employer name, rounded dates, generic institution type), watermark downloads, and rate-limit access.

## 7. What candidates expect from a free portal

### Findings

**Naukri's privacy and tracking features**
- **Block companies:** My Naukri → Settings → "Block companies". Candidates should block every spelling variant of a name (e.g. "ABC Pvt. Ltd" and "ABC Private Limited"). Naukri says it will "try our best... but we cannot guarantee". The profile can also be made fully private ([Naukri FAQ](https://www.naukri.com/faq/job-seeker-search-i-do-not-want-my-current-employer-to-have-access-to-my-profile.-how-can-i-block-a-recruiter%3F), [Naukri support](https://naukricom.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/228169-i-do-not-want-my-current-employer-to-have-access-to-my-resume-how-can-i-do-that-)).
- The advice to block name variants suggests the block works by **matching company-name text**, which is my inference. Blocking does not cover staffing agencies hiring for the blocked employer ([applycove](https://applycove.com/blog/hide-naukri-profile-from-current-employer/)).
- **Profile Performance** lists which recruiters acted on the profile: viewed it, downloaded the resume, viewed contact details, or emailed.
- Candidates can track **application status**, and get alerts when a recruiter views or shortlists them ([Naukri FAQ](https://www.naukri.com/faq/job-seeker-getting-around-naukri), [resume.naukri.com](https://resume.naukri.com/frequently-asked-questions-faq/how-do-i-view-the-details-of-recruiters-who-have-viewed-my-displayed-profile-on-your-website/), [Naukri blog](https://www.naukri.com/blog/greater-control-over-communication-from-naukri-com/amp/)).
- Confidence: High.

**LinkedIn**
- "Open to Work – recruiters only" is shown only to LinkedIn Recruiter users, and LinkedIn "takes steps" to hide it from recruiters at your current company but "can't guarantee complete privacy" ([LinkedIn help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/67405), [weekday](https://www.weekday.works/post/open-to-work-linkedin)).

**Ghosting**
- 73% of 1,000 Indian respondents said a potential employer had ghosted them (2024, [mediaindia](https://mediaindia.eu/society/silent-rejections-ghosting-disrupts-indias-job-market/)).
- Globally, 53% (iHire, Oct 2025, [iHire](https://www.ihire.com/resourcecenter/employer/pages/53-percent-of-job-seekers-have-been-ghosted-by-a-potential-employer)).
- No India survey for 2025–26: UNVERIFIED.
- Confidence: Medium-Low.

### Recommendation for our build
- **Block by company record, not name text.** Because the admin approves every company, we hold verified GSTIN/CIN records; block against those, including group companies.
- Suggest blocking the current employer automatically during onboarding.
- **Blocked companies must not see even the anonymised preview**, because an employer can recognise its own staff.
- Show candidates a timeline: matched → preview shown to N companies → unlocked by [company] → contacted → closed.
- Close each application with a status, and set a 30-day timeout that closes it automatically and tells the candidate.
