### High (20)

#### F8.24 · Jobboard300 staff cannot record what happened at an interview

- **Found by:** Functional auditor, in "8. Client: review the submission, shortlist, interviews and feedback" · **Type:** Gap
- **What happens:** Only the client can mark an interview completed, cancelled or no-show and record the outcome (`interview-record` exists on the client API only; the Recruiting tool has no such action or button). If the client tells the recruiter the result by phone, the recruiter cannot enter it, and the interview stays "scheduled" forever in both screens.
- **Expected:** A recruiter with Edit access records the outcome on the client's behalf (marked as recorded by Jobboard300), exactly as they can already schedule on the client's behalf.
- **How to fix:** Add `interview-record` to app/api/platform/recruiting/[action]/route.ts calling recordInterview() through a staff wrapper (pipelineJob scope check), and a "Record outcome" button beside each scheduled interview in recruiting.tsx.
- **Where:** app/api/platform/recruiting/[action]/route.ts; app/platform/recruiting/recruiting.tsx; lib/portal/staff.ts

#### F8.25 · An interview cannot be rescheduled

- **Found by:** Functional auditor, in "8. Client: review the submission, shortlist, interviews and feedback" · **Type:** Gap
- **What happens:** There is no reschedule action for the client or for staff. The only way to change the time is to mark the interview "Cancelled" (client only) and schedule a new one. The candidate then has two messages and is never told the first one was cancelled.
- **Expected:** Change date, time, mode or link on a scheduled interview; the candidate and the other side get one "rescheduled to …" message.
- **How to fix:** Add `interview-reschedule` (client and staff) that updates scheduled_at/mode/location on a `scheduled` interview and calls notifyCandidate + notifyCompany.
- **Where:** lib/portal/client.ts; both pipeline screens

#### F8.26 · The candidate is not told when an interview is cancelled

- **Found by:** Functional auditor, in "8. Client: review the submission, shortlist, interviews and feedback" · **Type:** Gap
- **What happens:** The client marks an interview "Cancelled". It is stored, but the candidate's inbox still shows only "Interview for … HR, <date>". Nothing tells them it is off, and the recruiter gets no update either.
- **Expected:** Cancelling sends the candidate "Your HR interview on <date> has been cancelled; your recruiter will contact you" and adds a line to the recruiter's view.
- **How to fix:** In recordInterview() (lib/portal/client.ts), when status is `cancelled`, call notifyCandidate(..., "interview.cancelled", …).
- **Where:** lib/portal/client.ts recordInterview

#### F8.27 · An interview outcome of "Do not proceed" does not move the candidate or tell anyone

- **Found by:** Functional auditor, in "8. Client: review the submission, shortlist, interviews and feedback" · **Type:** Gap
- **What happens:** The client records an interview as completed with outcome "Do not proceed". The candidate stays at "Interviewing" in every screen, later interview rounds stay scheduled, the candidate is not told, and the recruiter is not prompted. The decision has to be entered a second time through "Move to… Not selected" with a reason.
- **Expected:** Saving "Do not proceed" should either move the candidate to "Not selected" in the same step (asking for the reason code) or clearly prompt "Move this candidate to Not selected?". Likewise "Move forward" should offer the next step (schedule next round / make offer).
- **How to fix:** In the client FeedbackForm, when outcome is `reject`, show the reason select and call pipeline-stage → rejected after interview-record succeeds (one button). Server-side, recordInterview() can return `suggest: "rejected"` so both screens prompt.
- **Where:** app/company/company-app.tsx FeedbackForm; lib/portal/client.ts recordInterview

#### F9.19 · A job stays live after its only opening is filled

- **Found by:** Functional auditor, in "9. Selected: offer -> commitment -> offer accepted -> joined -> placement and fee" · **Type:** Gap
- **What happens:** The job has 1 opening and 1 person joined. It is still "live": it stays in the recruiter's job list ("0 in process · 1 joined"), recruiters can keep submitting new candidates to it, and (for a public role) the website keeps taking applications. Nobody is prompted to close it.
- **Expected:** When joined = openings: mark the job "Filled" (or prompt the client and recruiter to close it), stop new submissions, and take the public listing down.
- **How to fix:** After the placement insert in moveStage(), compare COUNT(joined) with jds.openings; if filled, set jds.status='closed' (or a new 'filled'), call syncRoleStatus for a public role, and notify the client and team. Show "n of m filled" on both job lists.
- **Where:** lib/portal/client.ts moveStage (stage === "joined")

#### F10.3b · A placement can be recorded with no salary and therefore no fee, and it cannot be corrected afterwards

- **Found by:** Functional auditor, in "10. Other outcomes: offer declined, did not join, withdrawn, early exit, replacement" · **Type:** Defect
- **What happens:** The offered salary is optional on both the client's and the recruiter's "Offer made" form, and "Joined" asks only for a date. An offer saved without a salary and then marked joined produced a placement with salary empty and fee empty (at 8.33%). No screen can edit a placement's salary or fee later: the owner console changes only its status and note. The fee is the whole revenue of the placement-fee model.
- **Expected:** The salary is required before a candidate can be marked "Joined" (ask for it on the Joined form if it was not given at offer), and the owner can correct a placement's salary and fee with a reason.
- **How to fix:** In moveStage() (lib/portal/client.ts) refuse `joined` when offer_ctc_inr is null unless the request carries it; add the field to both Joined forms; add a `placement-edit` owner action that updates ctc_inr and recomputes fee_inr, with an audit entry.
- **Where:** lib/portal/client.ts moveStage; app/company/company-app.tsx StageForm; app/platform/recruiting/recruiting.tsx; app/api/admin/portal/[action]/route.ts

#### F10.8 · A replacement that is due is not tracked anywhere a recruiter works

- **Found by:** Functional auditor, in "10. Other outcomes: offer declined, did not join, withdrawn, early exit, replacement" · **Type:** Gap
