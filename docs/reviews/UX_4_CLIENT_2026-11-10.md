# Client (hiring company) workflow: UI/UX review by DMAIC

Reviewed 10 November 2026 in the running local app, signed in as the test client owner, and in the code. One workflow only: accept the invitation, sign in, then the hiring workspace at `/company`.

**How to read this.** "Run" means I saw it on the screen. "Code" means I read it and did not exercise it. References such as F13.5 or U8.5 point to the audit report of 10 November; those items are not repeated as new, only built upon. My own findings are numbered C1 to C16 and my proposals P1 to P16.

**What my testing left behind.**
- One test job, "UX review test: Stores Assistant", was created, submitted and then closed. It remains as a closed record for the test client.
- One click on "Profile" wrote 42 entries to the access log and used 42 of the test owner's 60 profile views for the hour (see C1). No abuse flag was raised.
- The pipeline forms and the profile screen were rendered with stand-in data in the browser, because the test client has no candidate in process on an open job. Nothing was saved from them, so the click counts for the hiring steps were not timed against saved data.
- One exit report was refused by the server and nothing was stored.

---

## 1. Define

**Who:** the owner or HR person at a hiring company, and colleagues they add.

**What they want:** post a vacancy, see the people Jobboard300 puts forward, decide quickly, arrange interviews, make an offer, and confirm who joined.

**Done for the client:** the right person joined, with few visits to the screen.

**Done for Jobboard300:** every decision is recorded with a reason, the salary and joining date are captured (the fee and the replacement period depend on them), and CV access is logged.

## 2. Measure: the path as it is today

### 2.1 Getting in

| Step | Screen | Fields | Clicks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open the one-time link from Jobboard300 or the company owner | "Set up your hiring account" | Password (required, 12+ characters), Repeat password (required) | 1 |
| Confirmation "Your password is set" | same screen | none | 1 ("Sign in") |
| Sign in | "Sign in" | Email (required), Password (required) | 1 |

Total: 3 screens, 4 fields, 3 clicks. The password is typed three times. No terms or data-use statement is shown or ticked.

### 2.2 The workspace

One page with four tabs (Overview, Jobs, Pipeline, Team) and Sign out.

**Overview** (no fields):
- Five tiles: candidates in process, interviews in 14 days, offers awaiting joining, hires, your terms.
- "Updates from Jobboard300", unread only, with "Mark all as read".
- Hiring funnel of six tiles, interviews waiting for feedback, upcoming interviews, offers accepted and waiting to join, hires and replacement guarantee with "Report exit".

**Jobs:** two tiles (your terms, live jobs of allowed), the job list, "Post a job", and per job "Pipeline" and "Edit" or "Open".

### 2.3 Post a job (2 screens, 18 fields, at least 4 clicks)

Screen 1, "Post a job", 5 fields:

| Field | Required | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Job title | Yes | |
| Location | Yes | |
| Work mode | No | On-site |
| Openings | No | 1 |
| Job description (80+ characters) | Yes | |

Button: "Create draft".

Screen 2, the job editor, 13 fields, none required, all filled in from the description:
- Job title, must-have skills, essential (pass/fail) skills, nice-to-have skills.
- Minimum years, maximum years, cities, remote, accept relocation.
- Maximum notice, CTC from, CTC up to, minimum education.

Buttons: "Save changes", "Submit for approval", "Close job". After submitting, the person lands on Overview.

Three things are asked twice: the title, the location (then "Cities"), and the work mode (then "Remote").

### 2.4 Pipeline

Two filters: Job, and Show (In process, Closed, All). Each candidate row has "Profile", "Schedule interview" (when shortlisted or interviewing) and a "Move to…" list.

| Action | Fields | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Move to Shortlisted or Interviewing | Note | none |
| Move to Not selected, Withdrawn, Offer declined, Did not join | Reason, Note | Reason |
| Schedule interview | Round, Date and time, Mode (default Video), Link or address, Interviewer | Round, Date and time |
| Record feedback | What happened (pre-set "Interview completed"), Outcome (pre-set "Move forward"), Rating, Feedback | none |
| Move to Offer made | Offered annual CTC, Offer date (pre-set today), Note | none |
| Move to Offer accepted | Expected joining date, Last working day, Other offers, Note | Expected joining date |
| Move to Joined | Joined on (pre-set), Note | Joined on |
| Report exit (on Overview) | Two browser pop-ups: last working day typed as text, then reason | Last working day |

- Shortlisting one person after reading the profile takes 6 clicks: Pipeline, Profile, Back, open "Move to…", choose, Save.
- A full hire for one candidate passes through 6 separate small forms and roughly 16 clicks.

### 2.5 Profile and CV

Read-only: name, a notice that contact details come at offer, the trace line, experience, education, skills, and the pipeline stage. One button, "Download CV (watermarked PDF)", and one link, "Back to dashboard". There are no decision buttons.

### 2.6 Team

A table of people. "Add a colleague" has 2 required fields (Name, Work email) and one button. Per person: "New link" and "Remove" (with a confirmation). Only the owner sees these.

## 3. Analyse: defects and waste, most costly first

### C1. A failed screen load asks the server again without stopping, and on the Profile screen this locks the client out (Run). New.
- **What happens.** One click on "Profile" for a candidate whose profile could not be opened produced 42 logged profile views in about one second. The limit is 60 an hour. A client who leaves that screen open for two seconds is then told "You are viewing profiles too fast", cannot open any profile for an hour, and an abuse flag is raised against them in the owner console.
- **Other screens.** When the pipeline or team list fails to load, the page sends about 200 requests a second (403 and 437 in two seconds).
- **When it happens today.** Opening the profile of a candidate on a closed job (the stranded candidates of F13.5), a candidate who has just withdrawn consent, an expired session, or a network blip.
- **Root cause.** Each of these screens reloads whenever the message line changes, and a failure writes to the message line.
- **Who is affected.** The client is blocked and wrongly flagged. The owner gets false abuse flags and an access log full of noise. The candidate's access history, which is evidence for privacy, is distorted.
- **Where:** `app/company/company-app.tsx` (Pipeline, Team, the job editor and Candidate all load this way; the job editor was read, not run), `lib/portal/access.ts`.

### C2. "Submit for approval" throws away edits that were not saved first (Run). New.
- **What happens.** In the job editor I changed maximum notice from 30 to 45 and the top salary from 3.6 to 4, then pressed "Submit for approval". The job went for approval with 30 and 3.6. No warning was shown.
- **Root cause.** Two buttons sit side by side; Submit sends the stored version, not what is on the screen.
- **Who is affected.** The client believes a different job was posted. The owner approves the wrong requirements. The recruiter searches on them.

### C3. An invited client, and every colleague added later, never agrees to the conditions for handling CVs (Run and Code). New, and for your lawyer.
- **What happens.** The set-up screen has two password boxes and nothing else. The closed self-registration form does require a tick for "using candidate data only for hiring … and deleting CVs on request".
- **What is stored.** I found no acceptance stored for client users in the database. Only website enquiries and applications record a terms version.
- **Root cause.** The invitation path was added after the registration path, and the tick did not travel with it.
- **Who is affected.** Jobboard300, which hands watermarked CVs to people who agreed to nothing on screen. Candidates.

### C4. The Overview and the Pipeline disagree (Run). Builds on F13.5.
- Overview said "Candidates in process: 0" and "To review: 0" while the Pipeline listed one candidate waiting for review.
- The funnel said "Joined: 1" while the "Hires" tile said 2.
- The row that the Overview ignores is the same row whose "Profile" button fails and sets off C1.
- **Root cause.** The Overview leaves out closed jobs; the Pipeline does not.
- **Who is affected.** The client misses a candidate. The candidate waits for ever.

### C5. Updates bury the work (Run).
- Twelve unread updates pushed the hiring funnel to 1,419 pixels down on a desktop and 2,075 on a phone.
- Eight of the twelve read "New candidate for …", with no name and no link to the person.
- The only action is "Mark all as read". After that the updates are no longer shown anywhere (Code).
- **Root cause.** Updates are a flat list of text, not tied to the item they are about.

### C6. The funnel and the feedback buttons do not lead where they say (Run).
- All six funnel tiles open the same unfiltered list.
- "Joined" opens the "In process" list, where people who joined are never shown. They are under "Closed".
- "Record feedback" on the Overview opens the whole pipeline, not that interview.
- **Root cause.** The Overview passes no stage or person to the Pipeline, and "Joined" is filed as closed.

### C7. "Report exit" uses two bare browser pop-ups (Run).
- The date must be typed as text in the form YYYY-MM-DD.
- I typed 12/11/2026. The reason was still asked, and then the message was "Enter the last working day (on or after the joining date)", which does not name the real problem.
- There is no confirmation and the client cannot undo it, although a correct entry starts the replacement guarantee (see F10.8).
- **Root cause.** A shortcut was used instead of a form.
- **Who is affected.** The client. The owner, through a wrong guarantee status.

### C8. On a phone, two tabs scroll sideways and the header takes a quarter of the screen (Run). New: the audit did not check signed-in pages at phone width.
- At 390 pixels wide, Overview is 540 wide and Team is 527 wide.
- The header is 187 pixels tall.
- Jobs and Pipeline fit.

### C9. No decision can be taken where the decision is made (Run).
The profile has no "Shortlist" or "Not selected" button. The person reads the CV, goes back (losing the job filter, U8.5), finds the row again and opens "Move to…". That is 6 clicks for the most common action in the workspace.

### C10. Interview feedback is pre-filled with a positive answer (Run). Builds on F8.25 and F8.27.
- The form opens on "Interview completed" and "Move forward". Pressing Save without reading records a pass.
- The only way to cancel a future interview is hidden under a link called "Record feedback".
- Outcome and rating are still offered when "Candidate did not attend" is chosen. The server then discards the outcome (Code).
- **Who is affected.** The recruiter, who is meant to use the feedback to improve the next shortlist.

### C11. Two ways to reach "Interviewing", one of which misleads the candidate (Run for the screen, Code for the message).
"Move to… Interviewing" marks the stage with no interview behind it. The candidate is told "Check your inbox for the date and details", and there are none. "Schedule interview" already moves the stage by itself.

### C12. Posting a job: repeated questions and messages that do not match the screen (Run).
- Title, location and work mode are asked twice (section 2.3).
- The editor shows the raw words "Status: draft" and "Status: pending".
- On a brand-new draft it says "Editing a live job sends it back to Jobboard300 for approval".
- After submitting, the message says "Jobboard300 will review the points below" with no points shown, and the person lands on Overview rather than Jobs.
- The editor does not show the original description or the number of openings (R9), and has no "Back".
- On a job waiting for approval, "Save changes" takes it out of the approval queue back to draft, with nothing said (Code; F13.1 covers this for live jobs only).

### C13. Getting in costs more than it should (Run).
- An invalid or expired link still shows the password form. The person learns "This link is invalid or has expired" only after typing a password twice.
- The screen does not show which email the account is for.
- After setting the password the person must sign in again.
- The sign-in page offers "Create an account", which is the candidate form, and says nothing to a client whose link has expired.

### C14. Row clutter and a missing summary (Run).
- A new candidate's row shows "Submitted by Jobboard300" twice, side by side.
- The same badge then stays on every row for ever, although every candidate comes from Jobboard300.
- The row gives no experience, notice period or expected salary, so each first look costs a profile view, which is logged and counted against the limit.

### C15. Small wording errors (Code unless marked).
- "Median days from shortlist to offer" is measured from the day the candidate was submitted, not shortlisted.
- If a client's terms cannot be read, the tile still shows 8.33% and 60 days rather than nothing.
- "Back to dashboard" goes to the pipeline (U8.5, already known).

### C16. Messages do not say what happens next or by which channel (Run).
- "Moved to 'Offer made'." does not say that the candidate's contact details are now open to the client, or that the candidate is asked to confirm their commitment.
- "Interview scheduled." does not say how the candidate is told.
- This matters more now that you have decided confirmations go by email, and by WhatsApp with consent. Today client users have no phone number and no WhatsApp consent stored, so client updates by WhatsApp are not possible until both are collected (see P16).

## 4. Improve and Control: proposals

Each proposal gives the smallest change, the gain, the risk, and the control that proves nothing broke.

**P1 (for C1). Load each screen once; on failure show the message with a "Try again" button.**
- Gain: removes the lock-out and the false abuse flags.
- Risk: none to data. The rate limit and the access log stay exactly as they are.
- Control: a new check in `scripts/audit/uat.mjs`: open the profile of a candidate whose access has ended, wait two seconds, and count rows in `audit_events` with action `view_full`. Pass is 1 or 2, not dozens, and no row in `abuse_flags`. The existing test "a client cannot bulk-download…" in `tests/access-control.test.mjs` must still pass.

**P2 (for C2). "Submit for approval" saves what is on the screen first, then submits.**
- Gain: one click fewer, and no silent loss.
- Risk: saving replaces stored requirements, which today erases details the editor does not show (F13.2). Fix F13.2 first or with it, otherwise P2 makes that erasing more frequent.
- Control: extend the F13 checks in `scripts/audit/functional.mjs`: edit a value, submit, and read `jds.requirements` back with the new value and `jds.status = 'pending'`.

**P3 (for C3). Add one required tick box to the set-up screen, with the same statement the registration form already uses, and store when and which version each person accepted.**
- Gain: this adds one field. It is the only proposal that does, and it closes a legal gap.
- Risk: the wording needs your lawyer (R12 and R24 say the current text still talks of "unlocking"). People already invited would have to be asked once at their next sign-in.
- Control: a new column on the client user record (date and terms version), and a new test in `tests/access-control.test.mjs`: the candidate and download routes refuse a client user with no acceptance recorded.

**P4 (for C4). Count the same things in both places.**
- The smallest change: the Overview counts every open candidate, including those on closed jobs, until F13.5 closes them properly.
- Gain: no hidden candidate.
- Risk: the numbers change for clients with closed jobs.
- Control: a functional check that the sum of the funnel tiles equals the number of "In process" rows returned by the pipeline for the same client.

**P5 (for C5). Show at most three updates with "Show all", name the job and candidate, make each one open its item, and keep read updates under "Earlier".**
- Gain: the funnel returns to the first screen; one click from an update to the candidate.
- Risk: the candidate's name may appear only while the client still has access. Use the same rule the pipeline uses.
- Control: the existing check F8.23 (the client is told Jobboard300 scheduled an interview) still passes, and the `messages` rows are unchanged. This is display only.

**P6 (for C6). Each funnel tile opens the Pipeline filtered to that stage, "Record feedback" opens that interview's form, and "Joined" gets its own choice in the "Show" list.**
- Gain: 2 to 3 clicks per visit.
- Risk: none to data.
- Control: a UAT step: click "Joined" and expect the joined person's row.

**P7 (for C7). Replace the two pop-ups with a small form.**
- A date picker limited to the joining date up to today, an optional reason, and a confirm line that states the consequence: "This is inside the replacement guarantee" or "outside".
- Gain: no format errors, and an informed confirmation.
- Risk: none. The server check stays.
- Control: the existing exit checks around F10.8, plus `placements.status`, `left_on` and `note` filled as before.

**P8 (for C8). Let the tiles and the team table wrap or scroll inside their own box on narrow screens, and put the tabs on one compact row.**
- Risk: visual only.
- Control: extend the 360-pixel sideways-scroll check in `scripts/audit/quality.mjs` to the four client tabs.

**P9 (for C9). Put the row's own actions on the profile, and keep the job filter on Back.**
- Actions to add: Shortlist, Not selected, Schedule interview.
- Gain: shortlisting drops from 6 clicks to 3.
- Risk: the reason for "Not selected" must still be required, so reuse the same small form and do not make a one-click reject.
- Control: F8.28 (rejecting needs a reason) and U8.6 still pass, and `pipeline_events` gets its row.

**P10 (for C10). Remove the pre-selected positive answer and separate cancelling from feedback.**
- Outcome starts on "Choose…" and is required when the interview was completed.
- Outcome and rating are hidden for "did not attend" and "cancelled".
- A separate "Cancel interview" link is shown on future interviews.
- Gain: honest data. The click count is unchanged.
- Risk: one more required choice.
- Control: U8.8 still passes, the `interviews.outcome` values are unchanged, and a new check that an empty outcome is refused for a completed interview.

**P11 (for C11). Take "Interviewing" out of the client's "Move to…" list. Scheduling an interview already moves the stage.**
- Gain: one fewer choice, and no empty interview message to the candidate.
- Risk: a client who interviewed outside the system needs a path. Keep it possible by scheduling with a past time once F8.18 is decided, or leave "Interviewing" for staff only.
- Control: the U8.7 check that the stage is "interview" after scheduling.

**P12 (for C12). Stop asking twice, without removing anything stored.**
- Keep screen 1 as it is.
- On screen 2, show "What we read from your description" as a summary with an "Edit" link that opens the same 13 fields.
- Show the original description and the openings.
- Use plain status words.
- Print the points the server already returns, or drop "below" from the message.
- Land on Jobs after submitting.
- Gain: for a description that was read correctly, the 13 fields are seen but not handled. One screen of reading instead of a form.
- Risk: clients may skip checking. The owner's approval remains the control, so keep it.
- Control: F13.1 to F13.3, and `jds.requirements` identical before and after when nothing was edited.

**P13 (for C13). Check the link when the page opens, show the email, and sign the person in after the password is set.**
- An expired or invalid link shows the message straight away.
- The screen says "Setting up the account for name@company".
- After the password is set, the person goes straight to the workspace.
- The sign-in page says "Hiring companies join by invitation".
- Gain: fields from 4 to 2, clicks from 3 to 1, password typed twice instead of three times.
- Risk: signing in from a one-time link is acceptable only because the link is single-use and the person has just set the password. Keep the rule that the link is spent on use. The link check must not reveal whether an email exists.
- Control: the audit's "one-time link, used once only" check still passes, and a `portal_sessions` row is created only after the token is marked used.

**P14 (for C14). Show the stage once, drop the permanent badge, and add one line of summary to each row.**
- The summary line: years of experience, notice period, expected salary.
- Gain: fewer profile opens.
- Risk: the summary must follow the same access rule as the profile (shown only while the process is open) and must never include contact details.
- Control: the existing isolation tests in `tests/isolation.test.mjs` and `tests/access-control.test.mjs`, plus a check that a closed row carries no summary.

**P15 (for C15). Correct the label to "from submission to offer", and show a dash when terms are not available.**
- Risk: none.

**P16 (for C16). End each success message with the next step and the channel.**
- For example: "Offer recorded. Their phone and email are now on the profile. The candidate has been asked to confirm."
- When email is switched on (U9.9), send the client the same updates by email.
- WhatsApp to client users only after a phone number and a WhatsApp consent are collected per person. Neither exists for client users today.
- Risk: none to data, but a message must never promise a channel that is not connected.
- Control: the U9.9 check, and a new check that no WhatsApp message is queued for a client user without consent.

### Your decisions, reviewed for risk as they touch this workflow

- **Decision 3, the enquiry no longer asks for the job.** The client workspace's "Post a job" becomes the first and only place a job description is captured. That raises the cost of C2 and C12, so fix those before the new enquiry form goes live. The owner creating a client from an enquiry will also have no role details to carry over (U2.2).
- **Decision 4, the fee and replacement period stay off candidate-facing and home-page strips.** The "Your terms" tile in the client workspace is a signed-in employer screen showing that client's own terms. It is in line with the decision and should stay.
- **Decision 5, header and footer.** The workspace has its own header and is not affected. After sign-out it goes to `/portal/sign-in`, which matches the new links.

## 5. What to leave alone, and why

1. **Contact details held back until offer, and the notice that says so.** This protects the fee and the candidate. Only the wording about who arranges interviews needs settling (R7).
2. **The trace line on the profile and the watermark on every downloaded CV.** This is the legal statement and the deterrent. Do not shorten it.
3. **The limit on profile views and downloads, and the access log.** P1 fixes the screen. The limit itself must not be loosened.
4. **A required reason for every closed outcome.** It costs one choice and feeds the next shortlist.
5. **Owner approval of every job,** and the limit on how often a live job's requirements can change.
6. **Closed-out candidates shown only as a reference,** with no name, note or CV.
7. **Only the owner can add or remove colleagues,** with the confirmation on Remove. One-time links are valid 7 days and spent on use.
8. **"Joined" refuses a future date, and "Offer accepted" requires the expected joining date.** The placement and the follow-up list depend on both.
9. **The joining follow-up table and its check-in labels.** It is the one place the client is told what to do next without asking.
10. **The Team form.** Two fields, both needed.
11. **The "Your terms" tile** (see Decision 4 above).
